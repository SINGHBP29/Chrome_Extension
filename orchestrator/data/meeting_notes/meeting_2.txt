Date: Apr 15, 2026
Title: Capstone Ownership and Internship Orientation
Participants: HR Team, Internship Program Managers, Engineering Mentors

Summary:
The meeting focused on clarifying capstone ownership, intern onboarding flow, and how project expectations should be explained during orientation.

Discussion Points:
- Capstone projects are often inherited from prior intern batches.
- Current confusion exists about who approves project topics.
- Program managers requested a single ownership matrix for all tracks.
- Intern orientation deck lacks clarity on technical deliverables.
- HR requested simpler language for non-engineering stakeholders.

Intern Communication:
- On day one, interns should receive project context, scope, and success criteria.
- Ownership and escalation contacts must be listed directly in onboarding mail.
- Weekly sync schedule should be shared at the start of internship.

Risks:
- Delayed clarity leads to duplicated work between intern teams.
- Mentors are spending extra time correcting misunderstood requirements.

Decisions:
- Publish a capstone ownership document by team and domain.
- Add a dedicated orientation slide for "project assignment process."
- Include previous-year examples to reduce ambiguity.

Next Steps:
- HR to update onboarding communication template.
- Engineering mentors to submit finalized project ownership list.
- Program manager to create FAQ for capstone assignment and scope changes.
