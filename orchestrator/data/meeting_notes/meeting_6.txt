Date: Apr 19, 2026
Title: Weekly HR Sync - Intern Program Operations
Participants: HR Team, Program Managers

Summary:
Weekly sync reviewed intern program operations including attendance, timesheet discipline, engagement activities, and documentation consistency.

Discussion Points:
- Timesheet Submission:
  - Many interns missed weekly submission deadlines.
  - Team requested stricter tracking and reminder automation.
- Weekly Meetings:
  - Attendance quality needs improvement.
  - Program managers requested pre-meeting notifications.
- Engagement Activities:
  - Zumba session introduced as stress-relief activity.
  - Team-building activities planned monthly.
- New Joiner Welcome:
  - HR will introduce new interns with pictures.
  - Announcement will be posted via internal dashboard.
- Meeting Notes Documentation:
  - All meetings must be documented in centralized format.
  - Retrieval should be easy through semantic search.

Challenges:
- Inconsistent note quality across teams.
- Optional sessions have low participation in some cohorts.

Decisions:
- Chrome extension will support:
  - Session reminders
  - Notes popups
  - Timesheet alerts
- HR and engineering will jointly review weekly adoption metrics.

Next Steps:
- Engineering to implement reminder and alert features.
- HR to monitor attendance and participation trends.
- Program managers to enforce timesheet compliance checkpoints.
